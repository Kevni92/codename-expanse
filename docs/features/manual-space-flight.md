## Purpose

Provide the complete player-facing capability to directly pilot a ship through physical space, with optional assistance that remains subordinate to the approved physical simulation.

This feature composes player control intent, ship physics and Flight Assist. It does not define keyboard bindings, rendering, HUD styling or autopilot route planning.

## Player Capability

The player can directly command translational and rotational ship movement, choose whether Flight Assist is active, invoke supported assisted-flight actions, and regain manual authority over the ship without bypassing the physical simulation.

## Scope

- Manual translational and rotational ship control.
- Interpretation of player flight intent into commands consumed by the flight systems.
- Flight Assist activation/deactivation and supported manual-flight assistance.
- Manual authority and control handoff where assisted or automated systems are involved.
- Continuous use of the authoritative Flight Physics model.

## Out of Scope

- Autopilot route planning and autonomous travel execution.
- Navigation-target selection and target actions.
- Final keyboard/mouse/controller binding layout as an implementation detail.
- Final HUD or presentation design.
- Ship construction, modules or engine-fitting systems.

## Feature Composition

Player Flight Controls owns the meaning of player flight commands. Flight Physics executes the underlying physical response. When Flight Assist is active or an assisted action is requested, Flight Assist modifies or supplements control output according to its approved rules while preserving the same physical model.

The feature therefore has a clear flow: player intent is interpreted by the controls concept, assistance is applied where requested and allowed, and Flight Physics remains the authoritative source of resulting ship motion.

## Cross-Concept Interaction Flows

### Direct Manual Flight

1. [Player Flight Controls](../concepts/player-flight-controls.md) interprets player intent as manual flight commands.
2. [Flight Physics](../concepts/flight-physics.md) applies those commands through the ship's available physical capabilities.
3. The resulting physical state remains authoritative regardless of visual presentation.

### Assisted Manual Flight

1. Player Flight Controls expresses manual intent and the desired assist state/action.
2. [Flight Assist](../concepts/flight-assist.md) applies its approved stabilization, braking or holding behaviour without replacing the physical model.
3. Flight Physics executes the resulting forces/torques and updates the ship state.

### Manual Takeover

1. A manual command is generated by Player Flight Controls.
2. Any relevant automated-flight handoff follows the ownership rules of the automated system, including the approved Autopilot cancellation behaviour where applicable.
3. Manual Space Flight resumes control using the ship's current physical state rather than resetting motion.

## Ownership and Rule Boundaries

| Interaction / Responsibility | Owning Concept | Consuming Concept(s) | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Physical movement and rotation | [Flight Physics](../concepts/flight-physics.md) | Flight Assist, Player Flight Controls | No control mode may replace the authoritative physical model. |
| Assisted stabilization/braking/holding | [Flight Assist](../concepts/flight-assist.md) | Player Flight Controls | Assist behaviour is already defined by its concept. |
| Meaning of player manual-flight commands | [Player Flight Controls](../concepts/player-flight-controls.md) | Flight Physics, Flight Assist | Must be defined independently of a specific device binding implementation. |
| Autopilot cancellation on manual input | [Autopilot](../concepts/autopilot.md) | Player Flight Controls | Autopilot remains owner of its cancellation/handoff semantics. |

## Feature-Level Edge Cases

- Switching Flight Assist on or off must preserve the ship's current physical state and follow the approved Flight Assist reference-state rules.
- Manual takeover from an automated mode must not reset velocity, rotation or position.
- Insufficient ship control authority remains a physical/assist condition, not a reason for the control layer to invent hidden capabilities.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] The player can manually command translational and rotational flight through the approved physical model.
- [ ] The player can enable/disable Flight Assist and invoke supported assisted-flight actions without bypassing Flight Physics.
- [ ] Manual takeover preserves the current physical state of the ship.
- [ ] Responsibility for control intent, assistance and physical response is unambiguous between the required concepts.
- [ ] No required gameplay behaviour is defined only in this feature document without a concept owner.
